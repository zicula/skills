This proposal outlines a 90-day one-to-one coaching engagement. It covers what you get, how we work together, the investment, and the terms. Acceptance is one click at the end of this page.

1. Where you are now

You are stepping into a bigger role — more people looking to you, more decisions that matter, and less time to think. The gap is rarely information; it is focus, accountability, and a sounding board that is fully in your corner. That is what this engagement provides.

2. What the engagement includes

  • Twelve 60-minute private sessions — weekly for weeks 1–6, bi-weekly for weeks 7–12, scheduled at fixed times that survive calendars.
  • A written session summary after every call — decisions made, commitments taken, and what we watch for next — so progress compounds instead of evaporating.
  • Unlimited between-session support by email or voice notes — responses within one business day, for the moments a decision cannot wait for the next call.
  • One 360° stakeholder check-in (week 5) — a short, structured input from 3–5 colleagues so your development is grounded in how others experience you.
  • A closing review (week 12) — what changed, what to keep building, and a 6-month self-management plan.

3. How we work

Sessions run by video call. You set the agenda; I bring structure, honest observation, and the questions you have been avoiding. Between sessions you run small experiments in the real work — the unit of progress is a decision shipped, not an insight admired. Everything you share stays confidential; the only exceptions are the ones law and professional ethics require.

4. Schedule and commitment

The engagement runs for 90 days from our first session. Sessions can be rescheduled with 24 hours' notice; unused sessions expire 30 days after the engagement ends. The commitment works in both directions — I hold your slots exclusively for you.
