# Step 1 — Opportunity Intake Form (15 minutes)

Fill this the day the RFP lands, before reading it in depth. Its job is to capture the
facts that decide everything else: the real deadline, the mandatory qualifications, and
who owns the response. Copy this file, rename it to the opportunity, fill every field
with `___`. If a field needs research you do not have yet, write `UNKNOWN` — those are
your first questions for the buyer's contact (or the Q&A window).

often than in the writing.

## 6. First gut check (not the decision — that is Step 2)

Write three sentences: why this opportunity exists, why they might pick us, and what
scares you about it. The scorecard will test these against criteria; the win-themes
playbook (Step 6) will use the middle sentence.
