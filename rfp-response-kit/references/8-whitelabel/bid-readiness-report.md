# Bid Readiness Report — White-Label Client Template

**For agencies and consultants:** this is the client-facing deliverable for a bid
readiness assessment — the service where you evaluate a client's draft response (or
their capacity to respond) against the RFP, and hand back a structured report they can
act on. Replace every `[BRACKET]`, add your logo to the header, and deliver it under
your own brand. The kit files remain yours to work with; the report you produce is your
work product.

**How to run the assessment this report reports on:** shred the client's target RFP with
`2-shred/shredding-matrix.xlsx`, score the draft (or the plan) against it, run the three
red-team rounds from `6-review/red-team-checklist.md`, and summarize what you found
below. Report length discipline: 4–8 pages. Clients act on short reports.

## 7. Method and limitations

Assessment method: requirements matrix derived from the RFP; three-round review
(compliance, craft, persuasion); scoring simulation against the RFP's stated evaluation
criteria. This report assesses the response document and process — it does not assess
the underlying offer, pricing strategy, or the client's legal and regulatory
obligations, which remain with the client and their advisers.
