# 00 — START HERE

**RFP Response Kit — the operating system for a small bid room.**

This kit gives you one repeatable process for every RFP, RFQ or RFI you answer:

```
 intake ──► go/no-go ──► shred ──► win themes ──► draft ──► review ──► submit ──► debrief
 (15 min)   (30 min)     (2-4 h)    (1 h)         (days)     (half day)  (half day)  (1 h)
```

Eight steps. The same steps every time. Teams that answer 2–4 RFPs a month do not lose
because they cannot write — they lose because each bid is improvised: requirements get
missed, the go/no-go decision is a gut feeling, good answers from last bid are lost in old
documents, and nobody reviews the draft the way an evaluator will score it. This kit
replaces the improvisation with files you fill in.

## The two ways to use this kit

**Track R — Rush** ("the RFP is due in two weeks and I am the only one working on it")
→ Do Steps 1–2 today (30 minutes combined), then jump into the matrix and the outline
that matches your deal. `SETUP.md` walks you through it file by file.

**Track S — System** ("we answer bids regularly and I want this to be a machine")
→ Build the library first (Step 4's folder), then run one bid through the full 8 steps.
After two bids you will feel the compounding: each debrief feeds the library, and each
scorecard run sharpens which bids deserve your hours.

## Your first hour (Track S and Track R both start here)

1. Open `README.md` (two minutes — folder map and honest scope).
2. Fill `1-intake-and-score/opportunity-intake-form.md` for the bid on your desk (15 min).
3. Open `bid-no-bid-scorecard.xlsx` in your spreadsheet app and score it (20 min).
   The guide next to it explains every criterion and shows a worked example.
4. Look at the verdict cell. Either you commit the hours — and Step 3 (shred) is tonight's
   job — or you send the decline email from the guide and get your week back.

## The week, if you commit

```
 Day 1  intake + scorecard + decline-or-commit     (1 h)
 Day 2  shred the whole RFP into the matrix        (2-4 h)
 Day 3  win themes + pick outline + exec summary   (2 h)
 Day 4-8  draft sections from the library          (the bulk — parallel if you have a team)
 Day 9  red team: 3 review rounds                  (half day)
 Day 10 submission day checklist → submit early    (half day buffer)
 Later  debrief within 5 days of the decision      (1 h)
```

Deadlines vary; the order does not. Compliance first, persuasion last — a beautiful
document that misses requirement 14 loses to an ugly one that answers everything.

## The honest scope

This kit is process, templates and a starter library. It does not promise you wins —
win rates depend on your offer, your pricing and your market. It does not contain legal
or procurement-regulation advice: for complex government procurements with special
regulations, use procurement counsel; this kit manages *your own* documents and decisions.
The starter library is raw material written for small teams — every answer needs your
facts before it goes in a submission.
