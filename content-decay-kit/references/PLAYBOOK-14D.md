# PLAYBOOK-14D — from kit to a paid weekly deliverable

The kit's job is detection + the client-facing artifact. This playbook turns
the artifact into a line item. Fourteen days, part-time.

## Why clients pay for this

- Content decay is invisible until a client notices a lead dried up — the
  agency that reports it first owns the fix. A weekly digest converts a
  vague "SEO is down" call into a scoped, billable refresh task.
- The digest is a recurring, visible deliverable: every week the client sees
  your name on a document that shows work being watched. That is exactly the
  artifact that justifies a monthly retainer add-on.

## Price framing (copy-paste math for the pitch)

- Sell it as a care-plan add-on: **$29–99/month per client** is the common
  band for monitoring + a monthly/weekly report in agency care plans.
- One client at $49/month covers this kit in the first month. Everything
  after is margin on work you already do.
- Anchor honestly: all-in-one agency SEO suites run $129+/month; this kit
  costs ฿ once and runs on free infrastructure (Search Console's own data +
  your cron).

## Days 1–2 — set up + baseline

- SETUP.md end-to-end for 2–3 of your own clients. Run it, read the
  dashboard, tune `thresholds` until the flagged list matches your gut
  ("yes, that page did fall").
- Rebrand the digest (CUSTOMIZE.md): your name, your accent color, your
  digest title. Print one to PDF and look at it the way a client would.

## Days 3–5 — first sends

- Send this week's digest to 2–3 friendly clients with a three-line email:
  what it is, the one page you'd refresh first, what you need (usually: "can
  we get an hour this sprint?").
- Do the P1 refresh for one client. Note the before/after in next week's
  digest — the second digest with a "we refreshed /business-loan-guide, here
  is the recovery" row is the one that sells the retainer.

## Days 6–9 — productize the offer

- Write your one-pager: weekly digest + P1 refresh + monthly call = $X/month.
- Add it to your proposal template as an optional line item, not a question.
- Prepare the "found nothing" digest as a feature: quiet weeks are included;
  the watching is the product.

## Days 10–12 — extend to the portfolio

- Add the remaining properties (SETUP step 3 per client). Onboard a new
  client = add a config line + one Viewer invite. Time cost per client:
  ~3 minutes.
- Optional webhook → your team channel so P1s never wait a week.

## Days 13–14 — measure + decide

- Count: digests sent, refreshes scoped, care-plan upsells accepted.
- If ≥1 client accepted the add-on, raise the kit's cadence to all clients
  and fold the digest into every renewal conversation.
- If nobody bit after 3 honest weeks of digests, the detector is still doing
  its job internally — keep it, and revisit the pitch (usually the fix is
  scoping the refresh task, not the report).

## What this playbook deliberately does not promise

No promises of traffic or rankings, no "refresh always wins" — the
digest reports movement; your refreshes are scoped work with their own
outcomes. That honesty is part of the pitch: you watch, you report, you fix
what the data flags.
