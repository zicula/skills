# Pricing Setup — Reselling Reporting as a Service

How to turn this kit into a priced line item. The context first (verified against public
pricing pages, September 2026): dedicated reporting SaaS starts around **$44/month**
(DashThis entry tier, 3 dashboards) and AgencyAnalytics runs **$20 per client per month**
— an agency with 5 clients pays about $100/month, every month, for the platform alone.
Swydo starts at €69/month; ReportGarden at $75/month for 5 clients; Whatagraph from €699/month.
There is no meaningful low-end entry below ~$44/month on those platforms.

You don't need a platform to deliver a monthly report. You need a workflow — which is what
this kit gives you. That difference is the basis of the pricing below.

## The three pricing models (pick one as primary, offer one as fallback)

### Model A — Bundled into the retainer (most common for small agencies)
Reporting is part of the monthly retainer, never priced separately. When you raise retainers
or sign new clients, the report is already inside.
- **How to justify:** in the proposal, list "Monthly performance report (GA4 + Search
  Console, executive narrative, next-month plan)" as a named deliverable. Named deliverables
  anchor value; "monthly report" as a throwaway line doesn't.
- **Fit:** retainers from roughly $500–1,500/month per SME client — a full reporting
  workflow costs you 30–90 minutes once practiced, which is 5–10% of the hours a retainer
  at that level implies.
- **Rule:** never call it "free reporting". Call it *included*. Free devalues; included
  specifies.

### Model B — Line-item add-on (cleanest for existing client bases)
Offer reporting as an explicit add-on to clients you already serve.
- **How to price:** anchor against the SaaS alternative, not against your effort. The
  client can buy a reporting platform themselves from ~$44/month — but then they also do
  the reporting. Your fee covers the workflow *and* the interpretation.
- **A workable ladder (adjust to your market):**
  - **Essential** — monthly 5-section report (this kit), email delivery, quarterly call: price at roughly 1–3% of the client's monthly retainer, floor at a level that respects your 30–90 minutes.
  - **Pro** — Essential + a keyword-opportunity brief each month (Prompt 6) + a 30-minute walkthrough call: typically 1.5–2× Essential.
- **Rule:** always show the three things they get *above* the raw PDF — narrative in plain
  language, next-month plan, and a human who explains it.

### Model C — Standalone reporting retainer (for clients you don't otherwise serve)
Some businesses already have someone doing SEO/ads but nobody reporting on it.
- **Offer:** "Monthly performance reporting" as its own small retainer: the 5-section
  report, delivered on a fixed day, plus a short call.
- **How to price:** pick your local hourly rate for strategy work, multiply by 1.5–2 hours
  (your realistic per-client time after two months of practice), and round to a clean
  monthly number. Price it monthly, bill quarterly in advance.
- **Bound it:** cap it at data the client owns (GA4 + Search Console per this kit). Requests
  beyond scope are a new line, not a favor.

## Proposal language you can lift

- Deliverable framing: *"Every month you receive a five-section performance report —
  executive summary, traffic, search performance, conversions, and next month's plan —
  written in plain language, on a fixed delivery day."*
- Value framing: *"The report doesn't just show numbers; it explains what changed, why,
  and what we're doing about it next."*
- Annual framing (use at renewal): *"That's twelve decision documents a year — one per
  month — for less than the cost of one month of a dedicated reporting platform, without
  you operating any tool."*

## What not to do

- **Don't bill per hour for reporting.** You get faster every month; hourly pricing punishes
  your own efficiency. Price the deliverable.
- **Don't discount for "just the numbers".** A metrics dump is what free GA4 emails already
  do. You're paid for interpretation and the plan.
- **Don't promise outcomes in the report itself.** Expected effects are ranges tied to
  conditions (see template 05 rules). Promises in writing create liability and destroy trust
  when missed.
- **Don't expand scope silently.** Paid channels, social, email — each is a template and a
  fee away. Mention the boundary in the first delivery.

## The math to keep in mind

At one flat one-time cost for this kit and roughly 30–90 minutes of your time per client
per month, reporting costs you less per client than any monthly platform seat — and unlike
a platform seat, you keep the templates, the prompt library, and the workflow forever.
Two Essential-level reporting clients typically cover the kit's price many times over in
the first month; everything after that is margin you built an asset for.
