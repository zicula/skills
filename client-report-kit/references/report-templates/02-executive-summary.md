**Writing rules for this page**

- First sentence = the headline of the month ("Organic sessions grew 18.2%, driven almost entirely by the [guide] ranking #4 for [query]").
- Wins must carry numbers; "we did lots of work" is not a win.
- Watch items must already have an action attached — the client should never worry without seeing the plan.
- Max ~1 page when exported. Cut adjectives before cutting facts.
