**Filling rules**

- Percent-of-total is sessions-per-channel ÷ total sessions, 1 decimal, and the column sums to 100%.
- Keep GA4's channel names verbatim ("Organic Search", not "Google organic") so the client can verify in their own GA4.
- Mark any page with a tracking change (rename, redirect, new event) with a footnote symbol — unexplained cliffs destroy trust faster than bad numbers.
- If a channel row is trivially small (<1% share), keep it but don't narrate it.
