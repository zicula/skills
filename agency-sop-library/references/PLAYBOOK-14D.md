# PLAYBOOK-14D — Install Your Agency OS in 14 Days

You just bought 24 SOPs. This is the plan that turns them from files into your agency's operating system — without pausing client work. Budget **45–60 minutes per day**.

**The one rule:** install in the order below. Each day's SOP makes the next day's easier. Installing SOP-12 (revisions) before SOP-09 (comms) is how policies become arguments.

---

## Days 1–2 · Set up + personal numbers

- **Day 1:** Import the library (Notion *Text & Markdown* for full pages, or the CSV for a database — 10 minutes). Fill `[BRACKETS]` that repeat everywhere: your name, email, booking link, timezone (SOP-09's emergency channel, SOP-06's promise line).
- **Day 2:** Do SOP-04 once, on paper. Compute your floor rate and write the number somewhere you'll see it. Rebuild your last quote through its formula — did it clear the floor after the 1.3 multiplier? That answer tells you whether past margins were real.

**Done when:** library lives in your tool of choice + floor rate computed.

## Days 3–5 · Install the delivery spine (protects current clients first)

- **Day 3:** SOP-09 (comms contract) — send the short version to every active client today. Expect one "oh, that's smart" reply per client.
- **Day 4:** SOP-11 (weekly update) — pick your update day; send the first template-based update to every active project this week, at-risk protocol included.
- **Day 5:** SOP-12 (revision policy) + SOP-14 (change orders) — add the round definitions to active projects' docs; build your parking-lot list per client. The next "can you also…" gets the estimate email, same day.

**Done when:** every active client has: comms rules, an update day, and knows rounds are counted.

## Days 6–8 · Install the sales front (next lead meets the new machine)

- **Day 6:** SOP-01 (scorecard) — set your weights (you may keep ours), write your floor into it, and score every lead currently in your pipeline. Act on the scores: fast-track, nurture, or decline — this week.
- **Day 7:** SOP-02 + SOP-04's option table — rehearse the discovery script once out loud (seriously) and write your three-option skeleton (SOP-03) so the next proposal is assembly, not writing.
- **Day 8:** SOP-05 cadence — put every open proposal into the day-3/7/14 calendar right now. Anything older than 14 days gets the close-the-loop email today.

**Done when:** pipeline has scores, dates, and no "waiting to hear back" items.

## Days 9–10 · Install onboarding (for the next signed client)

- **Day 9:** SOP-06 (welcome packet) + SOP-08 (access checklist) — build both as templates with your links and stack. They ship together the day a contract signs.
- **Day 10:** SOP-07 (kickoff) + SOP-10 (plan) — agenda template with your timeboxes; milestone table format. Dry-run the kickoff agenda against your last real project: what would the risk block have said?

**Done when:** signing a client triggers a same-day packet + checklist with zero fresh thinking.

## Days 11–12 · Install QA + the retention loop

- **Day 11:** SOP-13 (QA) — pick your deliverable types, prune the checklists to yours, and apply to whatever ships this week. Add the QA line to your delivery email.
- **Day 12:** SOP-16 (monthly report) + SOP-19 (health scorecard) — build the report skeleton with your KPIs; score every retainer account once. Any 🔴 gets a call booked before the week ends.

**Done when:** next delivery is QA'd against a list; retainers have scores and a report date.

## Days 13–14 · Offboarding hooks + the operating rhythm

- **Day 13:** SOP-20/21/22/23 in one pass — set the pulse questions, build the handover checklist for your stack, write your testimonial ask + forwardable blurb. Offboarding now starts every project's finish line correctly.
- **Day 14:** Put the rhythm on the calendar — this is what makes it an OS instead of a good week: **weekly** update day (already set) · **monthly** first-Friday retainer scores (SOP-19) + reports (SOP-16) · **quarterly** QBRs (SOP-17) + alumni email (SOP-23). Recurring events, or it doesn't exist.

**Done when:** the calendar owns the rhythm, not your memory.

## The 30-day checkpoint (one month after Day 14)

| Signal | Meaning | Move |
|---|---|---|
| "How's it going?" emails → 0 | Comms spine installed | Next: SOP-17 QBRs for top accounts |
| A change order got *paid* | Scope leak plugged | Check SOP-14 bundle offer for accumulators |
| A lead got declined and you felt fine | Qualification working | Tighten SOP-01 weights with real conversion data |
| A 🔴 retainer got called and survived | Health system working | Start SOP-18 trigger log |
| Nothing above happened | You installed files, not habits | Re-run the week that failed; shrink to the first-6 SOPs (INDEX.md) |

## If you only have 2 hours a week

Install in this order, one SOP per week: **09 → 11 → 12 → 01 → 04 → 16.** These six carry most of the margin protection. The rest can wait a quarter — but they can't wait forever.

## Mindset notes (from agencies that ran this install)

- Expect exactly one awkward email per new policy. That email is the policy working — it's cheaper than the unspoken alternative.
- Don't customize before installing. Run the SOP as written for two real uses, *then* adapt. Premature customization is procrastination with a formatting excuse.
- The library pays for itself the first time SOP-12 or SOP-14 catches one unpaid "quick favor." Everything after that is margin.
