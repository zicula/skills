# SOP-01 · Lead Qualification Scorecard

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stage** | Sales |
| **Owner** | Whoever answers the inbox (founder by default) |
| **Trigger** | New inquiry arrives: form, email, referral, DM |
| **Time** | 10 minutes per lead |
| **Output** | Score 0–100 + one decided next action (fast-track / nurture / decline) |

## When to use

The moment a lead arrives — **before** you reply with enthusiasm. Bad-fit clients cost 3× their revenue in revisions, chasing, and stress. This scorecard takes 10 minutes and replaces gut feel ("they seemed nice") with a number you can defend.

Score every lead the same way, even referrals. Especially referrals.

## The scorecard (fill in per lead)

Six factors, 0–100. Score what the inquiry proves; anything unknown stays unknown — see the cap rule below.

| Factor | Points | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | 0–25 | States a range at/above your floor = 25 · vague ("depends on results") = 8 · states a number below your floor = 0 |
| **Authority** | 0–20 | Inquirer is the decision-maker = 20 · names a decision path ("partner signs off") = 12 · "gathering info for my boss" = 6 |
| **Need & timeline** | 0–20 | Concrete problem + date ("relaunch before the March expo") = 20 · real problem, "soon" = 12 · "exploring options" = 5 |
| **Fit** | 0–15 | Inside your stated niche/service = 15 · adjacent = 8 · outside = 3 |
| **Capacity** | 0–10 | You could start in their window = 10 · startable in 3+ weeks = 5 · you're full = 0 (say so honestly) |
| **Working signals** | 0–10 | Replies within 24h, answers directly, respects your process = 10 · one-word replies, wants a call *before* saying anything = 3 |

**Deductions, −10 each:** "cheapest quote wins" language · asks for spec work or a paid test task · reseller who hides the end client · NDA demanded before they'll say what the project is · already left 2+ agencies in a year (dig into why before scoring).

## Thresholds

| Score | Action | SLA |
|---|---|---|
| **70–100** | Fast-track: reply + booking link for discovery call (SOP-02) | Within 4 business hours |
| **40–69** | Nurture: reply with the two qualifying questions below; re-score on every reply | Within 1 business day |
| **0–39** | Polite decline + refer somewhere better-fit | Within 1 business day |

**The cap rule:** any *unknown* on Budget or Authority caps the lead at 59. Don't fast-track an unknown — ask, then re-score. This single rule kills most "great call, then total ghost" outcomes.

## Copy-paste assets

**Fast-track reply (70+):**

> Hi [NAME] — thanks for the detail in your note; it makes this easy.
>
> Short version: this looks like a fit. I do [SERVICE] for [NICHE], and [SPECIFIC THING THEY SAID] is exactly the problem [OUTCOME] solves.
>
> Two things before we book time so I don't waste yours:
> 1. What budget range are you working with? A rough range is fine.
> 2. Besides you, who weighs in on the final decision — and when do you want this live?
>
> If the answers are what I expect, here's my calendar for a 30-minute call this week: [BOOKING LINK]

**Two-question nurture (40–69):**

> Hi [NAME] — thanks for reaching out. Before I can tell you whether we're a fit, two quick questions:
>
> 1. What budget range are you working with? (It tells me what's realistic, and I'd rather be straight with you than waste your time.)
> 2. Who besides you signs off, and when are you hoping to start?
>
> With those two answers I'll give you a clear yes/no and a suggested next step — even if that step isn't me.

**Polite decline (0–39):**

> Hi [NAME] — thanks for thinking of me. Honestly, I'm not the right fit for this one: [REASON — e.g., "my floor for a site like this is $X, and it sounds like you're working below that"].
>
> Rather than leave you empty-handed: [REFERRAL NAME] does exactly this range and does it well — tell them [YOUR NAME] sent you.
>
> If scope or budget changes down the road, my door is open.

## Rules & red flags

- Score **before** the first reply, not after. First-reply enthusiasm is how boundaries die.
- Never skip the score for a referral. Write down who referred; score anyway.
- A 70+ score is permission to spend a discovery call, not an obligation to close. If the call reveals dealbreakers, you may decline later.
- Log score + action in your pipeline (a simple Notion database is enough). After ~20 leads you'll see which band actually converts — tighten the weights then.

## What good looks like

- 100% of inquiries have a logged score and a decided action within 1 business day.
- Every decline includes a referral. Goodwill compounds; declined leads send you better leads.
- 70+ leads convert to proposals at a visibly higher rate than 40–69 — that's the scorecard paying rent.
