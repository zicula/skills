# SOP-21 · Project Handover Checklist

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stage** | Offboarding |
| **Owner** | Project lead |
| **Trigger** | Final milestone accepted or contract end date within 14 days |
| **Time** | 2–3 hours spread over the final week |
| **Output** | A client who owns *everything* — accounts, files, docs, credentials — and a clean close that protects you legally and reputationally |

## When to use

Every project end, including endings you didn't choose (fired, budget cut, project paused). A graceful handover after a firing is remembered by everyone in their network — it's marketing (SOP-23 runs on this).

## Copy-paste: the checklist

**1. Accounts: transfer ownership, don't share.**
- [ ] Every platform where you created something: switch owner to the client, demote yourself to viewer (or remove)
- [ ] Analytics (GA4): client becomes account owner; keep a viewer seat only if they offer
- [ ] Ad accounts: client owns; remove your payment methods
- [ ] Domains/registrars bought on their behalf: push to their registrar account
- [ ] Social/tool admin seats: client admin, your seats removed
- Shared access expires; ownership survives. The test: **cancel your agency tomorrow — does anything break for them?**

**2. Files: they live in the client's storage, not yours.**
- [ ] Working files (design sources, docs, spreadsheets) copied to the client's drive/folder, with edit rights
- [ ] Final exports in the formats they'll actually need (SOP-13 list)
- [ ] Content calendar, brand assets, and photo library with a named-folder structure
- [ ] Email thread archive of decisions (a single exported PDF of the milestone approvals is enough)

**3. Documentation pack (the part clients never expect and always remember).**
- [ ] A 5–10 item "how this works" doc: where things live, how the site/flow was built, known quirks
- [ ] 2–3 short screen-recording videos (5 minutes each): "how to update X", "where Y lives", "what to do when Z"
- [ ] Vendor/third-party contact list (hosting, plugins, freelancers)
- [ ] Where passwords live now (their password manager — SOP-08 hygiene pays off here)

**4. Money and IP, in this order.**
- [ ] Final invoice sent; **IP/license transfer happens on final payment** (it's in your SOP-03 §7 terms — say it in the handover email: "full ownership transfers on settlement, per the agreement")
- [ ] Retainer/paused subscriptions in your name cancelled — set a calendar reminder for every one before you close
- [ ] Written statement of account: paid, outstanding, nothing hidden

**5. The closing email.**

> Subject: Handover complete — [PROJECT] is officially yours
>
> Hi [NAME] — everything is transferred. Attached: the handover doc (what lives where), the how-to videos [LINKS], and the statement of account.
>
> Three things that survive us: [SITE/CAMPAIGN] is fully in your accounts; the docs cover the 90% questions; and if something stumps you, email anyway — quick questions in the first 30 days are on the house.
>
> It was a pleasure building [OUTCOME] with you. When you get a moment in a few weeks, I'll ask you one small favor (it's a good one): [TEASE SOP-22 — testimonial ask].

## Rules & red flags

- Handover happens **even when the ending is ugly** — especially then. The fired-and-graceful agency gets referral calls within a year; the fired-and-petty agency gets a Reddit thread.
- Never hold files hostage over a disputed invoice — it converts a billing dispute into a legal and reputational incident. IP transfer timing per contract; hostile hostage-taking is never in the contract.
- Cancel every subscription you created *before* the final invoice, not "eventually" — a $29/mo tool leaking on your card for a dead project is the classic solo-agency tax.
- The "quick questions free for 30 days" line costs minutes and buys the SOP-22 testimonial and SOP-23 referral. Keep the window, honor it.

## What good looks like

- Zero "wait, you still have access to…" emails, ever.
- Handover pack delivered before final payment where trust is intact; simultaneously with it where it isn't.
- The client's next agency/vendor compliments the documentation — that compliment travels back to you.
