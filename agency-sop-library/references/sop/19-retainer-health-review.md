# SOP-19 · Retainer Health & Renewal Review

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stage** | Retention |
| **Owner** | Founder (for a 1–10 person agency, this is a founder job) |
| **Trigger** | Monthly, first Friday: review ALL retainer accounts · per-account deep dive 60 days before any renewal |
| **Time** | 10 minutes per account monthly · 45 minutes per renewal review |
| **Output** | Churn caught quarters early, renewals started 60 days out, save-attempts that are honest |

## When to use

On a fixed monthly date. Retainer churn for small agencies is almost never sudden — it's a slow sentiment drift that nobody scored. This SOP is a 10-minute monthly instrument panel. The rules: **usage numbers, sentiment signals, money dates — scored every month, no exceptions.**

## The monthly scorecard (per account, 10 minutes)

| Signal | Healthy 🟢 | Watch 🟡 | Alarm 🔴 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hours used vs. allocated** | 70–90% | >90% (scope creep risk) or 50–70% (downgrade risk) | <50% two months running |
| **Sentiment** | Client initiates; praise; forwards reports | Slower replies; shorter emails | "As per my last email"; boss cc'd; QBR deferred (SOP-17) |
| **Money** | Invoices paid on time | Paid after a nudge | Late twice or queries an invoice |
| **Strategic involvement** | Invited to planning; roadmap requested | Updates only answered | Cancels 1:1s; "just email me" |
| **Champions** | Contact responds + a second ally exists | Single point of contact | Your champion went quiet / changed jobs |

**Score:** 🟢 = 0, 🟡 = 1, 🔴 = 2 per row. **0–3 healthy · 4–7 act this month · 8+ fire drill.**

## Acting on the score

- **🟡 >90% usage:** pitch the bigger tier *now* (SOP-18 trigger #3 inverted — this is the good problem): *"We've used 95%+ two months running — you're outgrowing this tier."*
- **🟡/🔴 low usage:** call, don't email — *"We're using half the hours. Either we're scoping wrong or your priorities moved — which is it?"* A right-sized retainer survives; a padded one dies at renewal with resentment.
- **🔴 sentiment/money:** SOP-15 rung 2 call within the week. Name it kindly, fix it in writing.
- **🔴 champion left:** SOP-18 trigger #5 — re-anchor with the successor inside 2 weeks.

## The renewal deep dive (60 days out, 45 minutes)

1. **Evidence:** line up 3 monthly reports (SOP-16) + QBR recap (SOP-17) — the renewal case is already written; assemble it.
2. **Score trend:** last 3 monthly scorecards. A renewal pitched during a 🔴 month is a negotiation; during a 🟢 quarter it's a signature.
3. **The ask (in this order):** renew at current shape → expand (tie to a trigger, SOP-18) → multi-quarter discount for cash-flow certainty (your 10% floor rule from SOP-04 applies).
4. **If you don't want the renewal** (wrong-fit client, margin-burner): decline *yourself*, with a referral (SOP-01 muscle). Firing a bad client before they fire you is a growth move dressed as a loss.

## Copy-paste: the save attempt (score 8+, honest version)

> Hi [NAME] — shooting straight, because I'd rather fix this than lose it: from our side, the last two months look [USAGE/SIGNAL — e.g., "like we're solving fewer of your problems than the retainer promises: 40% hours used, two invoices queried"]. My guess is [HYPOTHESIS]. If I'm wrong, tell me how you'd score us. If I'm right, I'd propose [FIX — resize/reshape/pause 1 month]. 30 minutes this week — and if the honest answer is "it's not you, priorities moved", I'll help you wind down gracefully and hand over everything clean (SOP-21).

## Rules & red flags

- The scorecard takes 10 minutes *because* you track usage monthly. If hours-vs-allocated isn't measured, this SOP runs on vibes — fix that first.
- Never start a renewal conversation in the renewal month. 60 days is the minimum for a clean, unpressured yes (or a graceful, planned exit).
- A save attempt that opens with billing threats ("invoices are late, contract says…") ends the account. Open with the relationship; the contract is the last resort, in writing, via SOP-15 rung 4.
- One page per account per month, one folder, reviewed same day monthly. Boring rhythm is the entire trick.

## What good looks like

- Retainer churn below 10% annually — and every churn that does happen was forecast on this scorecard at least 60 days ahead.
- Renewals signed 30+ days before end date, half of them expanded.
- The phrase "they noticed before we said anything" — from a client, about a 🟡 month — is this SOP working perfectly.
