# Scope Alignment Worksheet

**Step 4 of the loop — ALIGN.** This is your internal alignment document. It
is **not an agreement and not a contract** — it records what both sides
understood on the kickoff call, so production starts from the same page. Your
contract (from your own lawyer) stays the binding document; this worksheet
keeps the daily work honest.

**Not written = not included.** That is the whole grammar of this file.

How to use:
1. Fill it in right after the kickoff recap goes out.
2. Send it to the client as the "scope summary" with one line on top:
   "This is our internal working doc — flag anything that looks wrong and
   I'll correct it in the thread."
3. Every change after this point flows through change-request-form.md.

### The three trades (when something must be added)

When a change request is approved, restore the balance by choosing one:

1. **Trade time** — move the milestone dates (update milestones.csv, tell the
   client which dates moved and why).
2. **Trade money** — fixed-price addition (the change request states the cost
   before work starts).
3. **Trade scope** — remove something of similar size, in writing, from the
   IN table above (nothing is silently dropped; the client sees the swap).

## The worked example, computed

Copperline, week three: "Can we add a simple events page? And Riley wants the
wholesale form to prefill from the price list."

| Request | Size | Fieldnote's response |
|---|---|---|
| Events page (template exists, new content type) | Small | Change request: +$600, launch date holds because page reuses the template. Dana approves by email — CR-001 logged. |
| Wholesale form prefills from price list | Medium | Requires the price-list system that is OUT by kickoff decision. Offered the trade: add it (+$1,400, +1 week) or keep hand-updated. Riley chose to keep hand-updated; logged in "Excluded by decision" with the date. |

Two asks, one approved with money, one declined with a written reason. Both
live in writing. That is the worksheet doing its job — the project timeline
survived both conversations.

## What success looks like

- The client has replied to the scope summary with either "looks right" or
  corrections — and the thread holds the final version.
- You can quote any in/out decision from week three by linking a document,
  not from memory.
- When new work appears, the client themselves says "can you change-request
  that?" — because the process has been visible since week one.
