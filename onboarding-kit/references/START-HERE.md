# 00 — START HERE

The Client Onboarding Kit is a single loop you run for every new client.
Read this page once (about 8 minutes), set up your copies of the files
(fill the [bracketed] blanks in the templates with your own details once —
see SKILL.md), and from then on each new client costs you
about 45 minutes of setup and 15 minutes a week.

## The loop (8 steps, in order)

```
CAPTURE  →  MEET  →  ALIGN  →  CONNECT  →  PLAN  →  SET RHYTHM  →  WORK  →  CLOSE
(intake)   (kickoff) (scope)  (access &   (mile-   (cadence &     (updates) (handoff)
                              assets)     stones)  welcome pack)
```

| Step | Files | When | Time budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. CAPTURE | 1-intake/new-client-intake-questionnaire.md, intake-response-log.csv | After the client says yes, before the kickoff call | 10 min to send, answers come back async |
| 2. MEET | 2-kickoff/kickoff-call-agenda.md, kickoff-call-script.md | 30–60 minute kickoff call | 60 min (call) |

## Time budget, honestly

- **One-time setup (you, once):** about 90 minutes — read this folder, fill in
  the [bracketed] blanks in the templates with your own details (company name,
  response times, tool names), delete rows that do not apply to your business.
- **Per new client:** about 45 minutes of onboarding work, most of it before
  any billable production starts. The questionnaire runs async, so the real
  live cost is the kickoff call itself.
- **Per week during a project:** 5–10 minutes per client — one status update,
  one glance at the asset log.

## Two rules that make the whole kit work

1. **Nothing starts before CAPTURE and ALIGN are done.** The questionnaire and
   the scope worksheet are what protect your timeline later. A project that
   starts before scope is aligned is a project you will be re-explaining in
   week four.
2. **If it is not written, it is not included.** Every promise lives in one of
   these documents, in writing. Verbal agreements do not get tracked, so they
   do not count. (This is a process rule for your internal alignment — it does
   not replace your contract. See 10-scope-and-disclaimers.md.)

## Which file do I open right now?

- I just got a "yes" from a new client → `1-intake/new-client-intake-questionnaire.md`
- My kickoff call is tomorrow → `2-kickoff/kickoff-call-agenda.md`
- The client just asked for "one more small thing" → `5-communication/scope-creep-scripts.md`
- The project is wrapping up → `6-handoff/project-handoff-checklist.md`
- I want AI to draft my onboarding docs → `7-ai/ai-prompt-pack.md` (read the
  fact-check gate at the bottom first)
- I am not sure what this kit is for → `10-scope-and-disclaimers.md` (what the kit is and is not)
