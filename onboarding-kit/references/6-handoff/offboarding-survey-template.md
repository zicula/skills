# Offboarding Survey Template

Send this two days after handoff — close enough that everything is fresh,
late enough that the walkthrough dust has settled. Six questions, two minutes
for the client. Its outputs: one fix for your process, one testimonial
candidate, one follow-up conversation.

Rules:
- Six questions, not sixteen. Response rate is the metric that matters.
- The testimonial ask rides on their own words — you quote them back to
  themselves, and they only have to say "yes, use that."
- Every answer gets a written response from you within a week, even the
  neutral ones. The survey is also your last communication of the project —
  it sets the tone for the next one.

---

## The template

```
Subject: Two minutes to make the next project better, Dana?

Hi Dana,

Copperline is live and I want the next client (and you, if we work together
again) to get an even better run. Six quick questions — any answer is
useful:

1. Which part of working with us was most valuable?
2. Where did our process slow you down or confuse you?
3. Was anything unclear about scope, dates, or communication at any point?
4. If we changed one thing, what should it be?
5. On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to recommend us to another
   business owner? (10 = actively pushing your friends to hire us)
6. Anything else you want in the record?

Thanks — this genuinely shapes how we run projects.

Priya
```

## The testimonial ask (only if the answer is warm)

```
Subject: Your words, with permission

Hi Dana,

Your line — "I checked them every week instead of worrying" — is exactly
what we want people to know about working with us. May we use it as a
testimonial? You'd be quoted as "Dana Whitfield, Copperline Coffee Roasters"
alongside a line about the project (brand refresh + website). We'll show you
the final text before it goes anywhere, and using your words is entirely
your call.
```

Result (worked example): Dana approved with one edit. The final text quotes
her sentence about the Friday updates and nothing else — no embellishment
added by the studio. (Honesty is the policy here: quote what they wrote, add
context only, and show them the final.)

## After the survey

- [ ] Reply to every answer within a week (even "thanks — noted, and here's
      what we changed")
- [ ] Log the one process fix you adopted (one per project — the survey is a
      changelog input, not a suggestion box)
- [ ] Score 9–10 and warm words → testimonial ask + follow-up-project or
      referral conversation
- [ ] Score 7–8 → one question back: "what would have made it a 10?" and
      actually fix that thing
- [ ] Score 6 or below → a 15-minute call, offered genuinely, to hear the
      full version before you respond

## What success looks like

- Response rate above 50% (six questions, two minutes, two days after a good
  handoff).
- Every project ends with one adopted process fix — the kit itself evolves
  from these.
- Testimonials in your library quote clients' own sentences, and you can
  trace each one back to a survey answer.
