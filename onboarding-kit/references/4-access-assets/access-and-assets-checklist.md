# Access & Assets Checklist

**Step 5 of the loop — CONNECT.** Everything the project needs to touch must
arrive through a channel you control, in week one. This checklist has two
jobs: completeness (nothing stalls in week three because a login surfaced
late) and hygiene (you ask for invites and shares, not raw passwords).

**The hygiene rule, stated once:** ask clients to grant access through the
platform itself — admin invites, sharing links, transfer flows. If a system
has no sharing mechanism and a credential must be sent, ask them to send it
through their password manager's secure share, and ask them to rotate it when
the project ends. You are protecting them and yourself with the same move.
(This is process hygiene, not a security audit.)

## C. The one-page request email (send once, week one)

```
Subject: Copperline — access + assets I need this week

Hi Dana,

To keep our Nov 1 date honest, here is everything I need from your side,
with the latest date each item can arrive. Two minutes per item.

ACCESS (via platform invites — no passwords needed)
1. Cloudflare zone invite — by Wed Oct 8
2. Shopify admin invite (from [former developer]) — by Fri Oct 10
3. Mailchimp editor invite — by Wed Oct 8

ASSETS
4. Brand one-pager PDF — already received, thank you
5. 2024 photo originals — by Tue Oct 14 (this is the one that moves
   milestone M3 if late)
6. Final wholesale copy — by Tue Oct 14

If any date is a problem, tell me today and we'll plan around it — that is
much easier than finding out on the 14th.

Priya
```

## D. Weekly 2-minute sweep (during the project)

- Any `Received` cell still empty with a date inside 5 business days → named
  in Friday update under "decisions needed".
- Any new system that appeared mid-project → add a row, then treat it like
  the others (ask, date, receive).
- Access held by a third party → confirm the transfer happened, then mark the
  contact person "disengaged" so nobody waits on them again.

## What success looks like

- Week one ends with the table full of dates, not intentions.
- Zero passwords in your inbox — invites and shares only; anything that had
  to be sent directly is rotated at handoff (handoff checklist tracks this).
- When M3 review day comes, every asset it depends on arrived by its written
  date — or the delay was named in a Friday update the week it became real.
