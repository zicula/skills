# Kickoff Call Agenda (60 minutes)

**Step 2 of the loop — MEET.** This is the single live meeting of the
onboarding phase. The goal is narrow: leave with every open question answered,
every red flag from the questionnaire resolved, and next steps owned by name
with dates.

Rules for the meeting:
- Attendees: you, the client's owner, and anyone who must approve work.
- Send this agenda to the client the day before, so they can object or add.
- Timebox every segment. The agenda below sums to 60 minutes with a 5-minute
  buffer inside "Open floor".
- You are the timekeeper. Politely park off-topic items in "The parking list".

---

## The agenda

### 0:00–0:05 — Why we are here (5 min)

- One line on the project as you understand it.
- One line on the process: "You'll get a written recap of this call tomorrow,
  then a written scope summary this week. Both are short."
- Confirm the attendee list: who decides, who advises.

### 0:05–0:15 — Goals, restated (10 min)

- Read back the client's own answers to questions 8–14 of the questionnaire
  (why now, what changes in 6 months, the single most important outcome).
- Ask: "If we only achieve one thing in this project, which one is it?"
- Write their exact words down. This sentence becomes the objective line in
  the scope worksheet and the first line of the milestone plan.

### 0:15–0:30 — The red list (15 min)

Walk through every red and amber from the intake log. One item each:
what we saw, what it affects, what we propose.

*Worked example (Fieldnote × Copperline):*

| # | Item from intake | Affects | Proposed in call | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shopify admin is a former developer (Q32) | Week-one access | Dana sends him an invite-based admin transfer this week | Yes — Dana by Fri 2026-10-09 |
| 2 | Soft launch before Nov 1 (Q36) | Milestone plan | Treat Nov 1 as hard: work backwards from it | Yes — confirmed hard edge |
| 3 | Wholesale price list is one person's spreadsheet (Q34) | Handoff | Add "price list shared access" to handoff checklist | Yes — Riley + Dana |

### 0:30–0:45 — Scope boundary, out loud (15 min)

- Read the in-scope list as you currently understand it (3–4 bullets max).
- Then read the out-of-scope list. This is the part clients actually
  remember — say it plainly, in one breath each.
- Ask: "Anything you assumed was included that I just listed as not included?"
  If yes: resolve it now — move it in and name what comes out, or leave it out
  in writing. This question in this meeting is the cheapest scope-creep
  prevention you will ever get.
- Name the change process: "If new work shows up mid-project, that is fine —
  it goes through a one-page change request so the timeline stays honest."

### 0:45–0:55 — Working rhythm (10 min)

- Weekly updates: day, channel, what they will contain (5 lines — see
  status-update-template.md).
- Response-time expectations both ways (see cadence playbook).
- How to raise an urgent issue (one channel, not five).
- When the next check-in is.

### 0:55–1:00 — Close (5 min)

- Read back the three next actions, each with an owner and a date.
- Confirm the recap email arrives within 24 hours.
- Ask the last question of every kickoff: "What would have to be true for you
  to call this project a win?" Compare the answer with the 0:05 version. If
  they differ, resolve before you close the call.

---

## The parking list

Off-topic ideas go here, out loud, with an owner: "Good thought — parking it
for the wrap-up week / for a follow-up project." The list travels into the
kickoff recap email so nothing raised gets silently dropped.

*Worked example parking items:*
1. "Email template for the holiday campaign" — Dana — parked for follow-up
   project conversation at handoff.
2. "Loyalty punch-card design" — Dana — out of scope; revisit after site
   launch.

## What success looks like

- You leave with: zero open reds, a written-down one-thing-that-matters, an
  agreed scope boundary that was read out loud, a rhythm, and three owned
  next actions with dates.
- If any of those five is missing, the call is not done — book the remaining
  15 minutes within 48 hours rather than letting it drift.
