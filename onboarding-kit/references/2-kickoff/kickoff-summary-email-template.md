# Kickoff Summary Email (send within 24 hours)

**Step 3 of the loop.** The recap is where spoken agreements become written
ones. Send it the same or next day, while memory is fresh and before anyone's
recollection drifts.

Rules:
- Plain text or light formatting. It is a record, not a brochure.
- Every decision gets an owner and a date, in the same line.
- The out-of-scope list is copied verbatim from what you read on the call.
- End with the one-sentence win definition in the client's own words.

---

## The template

```
Subject: Copperline kickoff — recap + next steps (action by Fri)

Hi Dana,

Thanks for a sharp kickoff call. Here is everything we agreed, in writing.

WHAT WE'RE DOING
[One sentence: the project, in the client's words from the call.]
Worked example: "Brand refresh + 6-page website for Copperline, aimed at
growing online subscription sales, launching before Nov 1."

DECISIONS FROM TODAY
1. Shopify admin moves via invite from [previous developer] — Dana — by Fri Oct 9
2. Nov 1 soft launch is a hard date — everyone — plan works backwards from it
3. Wholesale price list gets shared edit access at handoff — Riley + Dana — at handoff

WHAT'S IN SCOPE
- [item 1 — e.g. brand refresh: logo refinement, palette, type, one-page guide]
- [item 2 — e.g. 6-page website: home, shop, subscriptions, wholesale, story, contact]
- [item 3 — e.g. analytics + newsletter hookup]

WHAT'S OUT OF SCOPE (unchanged unless we sign a change request)
- Email templates
- Packaging design
- Wholesale price-list system
- Photography

HOW WE WORK
- Friday updates from me, 5 lines: done / next / blocked / decisions needed / key dates
- Email for everything; mark URGENT for same-business-day response
- Approvals: if I need an answer by a date and it doesn't come, milestone
  dates shift by the same amount. You'll see it in the Friday update.

YOUR NEXT STEPS
1. Send Shopify admin invite — by Fri Oct 9 — Dana
2. Share the 2024 photo originals + 2023 brand PDF — by Wed Oct 7 — Dana

MY NEXT STEPS
1. Recap email (this one) — today — Priya
2. Scope summary + milestone plan — by Thu Oct 8 — Priya

WIN CONDITION, YOUR WORDS
"[The client's one-thing sentence.]"
Worked example: "Subscription signups go up without the brand feeling
generic."

Corrections welcome — if I wrote down something wrong, reply and I'll fix it
in the thread so we're all looking at the same version.

Priya
Fieldnote Studio
```

---

## Why each part earns its place

| Section | What it prevents |
|---|---|
| Decisions with owner+date on one line | "I thought you were doing that" |
| Out-of-scope copied verbatim | the week-four "I assumed that was included" |
| Response-time paragraph | silence treated as agreement, both directions |
| Win condition in client's words | success being redefined at the end |
| "Corrections welcome" line | the recap becoming an argument instead of a record |

## If the client goes quiet after the recap

One nudge, 48 hours later, one line: "Circling this to the top — if the recap
looks right, I'll start on the scope summary." Then proceed on the written
record you have. Two nudges and silence means the rhythm conversation needs
to happen again; see communication-cadence-playbook.md.

## What success looks like

The recap thread becomes the project's reference: when a question about scope
or dates comes up in week four, you reply with a link to this email, and that
is the whole conversation.
